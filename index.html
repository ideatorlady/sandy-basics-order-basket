How ordering works

1. Browse collections and choose your favorites.
2. Add items to your basket. On mobile, use the browser’s Back arrow (←) after adding an item to return to the collection.
3. Copy your order list, tap Take Me to Order ↗, and Paste the list into the message box.
4. Confirm availability, shipping costs, and your final total with Sandy Basics.
5. Pay using a PayPal request or invoice after confirmation. Your order is then prepared for shipping.

No payment is taken here. This is an order request, not a confirmed purchase.
